WHAT IT IS

Xerox factory distribution floppies for the 1108/1101 workstations, imaged
with ImageDisk in July 2018 (two disks carry an older IMD 1.17 header dated
28-Apr-1992).  70 .IMD images, 73 MB uncompressed, in five sets: LYRIC (21),
KOTO (21), LOOPS (13), HARMONY (10), SOURCE (5).

Stored packed.  The manifest below is every disk's own IMD header comment, so
the set can be searched without unzipping.

READING THEM (2026-08-11): tools/pilot_floppy.py does both layers -- the
ImageDisk container and the Xerox Pilot floppy volume -- so no emulator is
needed.  The Pilot record layouts come from Xerox's own Interlisp FLOPPY
package, chm/lisp-archive/koto/lisp/sources/FLOPPY!1 (Kelly Roach, 1985):
DATATYPE PSECTOR9 / PMPAGE / PLPAGE, BLOCKRECORD PFILELIST, DATATYPE PFLE.

  unzip -d /tmp/imd InterlispReleaseFloppyImsges.zip
  python3 tools/pilot_floppy.py list    /tmp/imd/*/*.IMD
  python3 tools/pilot_floppy.py extract OUTDIR /tmp/imd/*/*.IMD

Yields 5,594 files / 59,862,054 bytes across the 70 disks.  Nothing is
copied into the tree: the zip is here and the tool is in tools/, so the
extraction regenerates in seconds.

Note the internal Pilot volume labels are often better than the IMD header
comments -- 3100420 calls itself "Harmony LispUsers 1" (119 files) and
3100416 "Harmony Library 1" (69 files).

NOT IN HERE, despite the name

- No Medley.  The releases stop at Lyric and Koto; the Medley 1.0 library
  still exists nowhere we have found.  See docs/xerox-systems-archive-survey.md
  section 3 and the CHM inventory: [phylum]<Medley> is 22 files total
  (2 sysouts, 19 patches, 1 copylog) and has no library.
- No Dorado.  A Dorado has no floppy drive; these are 1108/1186 media.  The
  Lisp sysouts here are the 1108 sysouts -- and because Interlisp-D sysouts
  are machine-independent (which is why chm/lisp/lyric/LISP.SYSOUT!1 boots on
  our Dorado under DORADOLISPMC.EB), reassembling Lyric Sysout #1-5 would just
  reproduce a sysout we already hold from CHM.

WHY IT IS WORTH KEEPING

- LOOPS is the new runnable material: 1108/1101 LOOPS demo sysouts, the KB
  LOOPS sysouts, and LOOPS source + .DCOM.  CHM gives us indigo/loops (the
  1983-84 releases) but not these.
- Harmony "Users Packages" #1-2 -- CHM has only 8 files under
  eris/lisp/harmony/lispusers.
- Lyric "Library" #1-3 may carry the five .LCOM we lack (GRAPEVINE, MAINTAIN,
  MAILSCAVENGE, GIVE-AND-TAKE, MESATYPES), though those are a direct download
  from CHM and that is far cheaper than IMD extraction.

This is new material to us: our other floppy sets are a different container
AND a different part-number range -- chm/bitsavers/bits/Xerox/1186/
1186_floppies.zip is .dmk with generic 001_001-style names, and
1186_Lyric_Sources.zip is .dmk parts 3103240-3103250 (Lyric SOURCES).  The
Lyric disks here are 3102390-3102416, the Lyric binary release.
