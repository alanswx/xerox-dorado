How to read the Atlas

The archive inventory is evidence, not the main story. Start with an environment, follow its boot chain and media recipe, then inspect the raw CHM occurrences when you need provenance or missing files.

Occurrence means one CHM copy. Artifact means a meaningful input such as a germ, boot file, disk, or microcode world. Recipe means a tested or proposed way to assemble those artifacts into a working environment.
