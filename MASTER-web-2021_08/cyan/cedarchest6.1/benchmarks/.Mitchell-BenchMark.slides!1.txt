
Martin Richard's Benchmark
    Benchmark measures a language, compiler, machine combination
        simulates a small (Tripos-like) operating system
        schedules and executes six tasks:
        2 devices, 2 device handlers, working task, idle task
        driven by a pseudo-random number stream
        terminates after a certain amount of data processed
        checks that random number generator and simulation ran right
